Core proposition: 
Turn every Slick into searchable, structured, measurable operational knowledge. 

- Record or upload a Slick. 
- AI transcribes it. 
- AI suggests title, description, tags, goals and skills. 
- AI extracts key steps, risks and actions. 
- AI creates a checklist, SOP or markdown guide. 
- AI links it to roles, goals, skills and playlists. 
- Users can ask questions and get timestamped answers. 
- Managers can see knowledge gaps, skills evidence and impact.
